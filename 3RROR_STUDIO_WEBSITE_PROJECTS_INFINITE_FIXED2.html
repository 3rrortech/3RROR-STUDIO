Questions

Before You Book

Do I fully own the website after it's built?

Yes. Once your build is paid in full, the source files and full ownership transfer to you — no lock-in, no monthly fee required to keep it running.

What's the difference between Starter, Growth, and Pro?

Starter uses our pre-built premium components assembled around your business — fast and affordable. Growth and Pro are increasingly custom-coded from scratch, with more pages, integrations, and design work tailored to your brand.

Do you host the site, or do I need my own hosting?

We can set up hosting and your domain for you, or hand over the files if you'd rather use your own host — your call.

Can I make changes after the site is live?

Yes — either your own developer (since you own the code), or come back to us for updates and new features.

Do you take payments through PayFast, Yoco, or Ozow?

We can integrate local SA payment gateways like PayFast, Yoco, or Ozow into Growth and Pro builds if your business needs to take payments online.

3RROR STUDIO collects only the information you submit through our forms — name, email, phone, and project details — to respond to your request and deliver your build. We do not sell your data.
